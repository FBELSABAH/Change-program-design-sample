What participants do

This opening week introduces the CHANGE Program, explains how sustainable habit change works, and helps participants choose one nutrition goal and one movement goal.

  • Watch a short welcome video and review the participant guide.
  • Read the healthy plate and movement planning lesson.
  • Fill out a one-week habit tracker.
  • Complete the knowledge check below.

Week 1 knowledge check

Focus for the week

Participants practice meal timing, plate-building, and simple substitutions that support energy and satiety.

  • Review the grocery planning checklist.
  • Try one protein-forward breakfast idea.
  • Submit a two-sentence reflection in the forum.

Focus for the week

This module supports participants in choosing accessible movement routines and identifying common barriers before they interrupt progress.

  • Choose a walking, mobility, or strength-based option.
  • Schedule three short movement blocks.
  • Complete a confidence-rating self-check.

Focus for the week

Participants explore the connection between routine, recovery, and long-term change using practical strategies rather than all-or-nothing expectations.

  • Track one evening routine habit.
  • Use the stress-awareness worksheet.
  • Post one takeaway in the peer discussion area.
