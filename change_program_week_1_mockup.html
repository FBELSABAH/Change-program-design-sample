CHANGE Program: Week 1 Module

A sample online-learning module mockup showing how print-based wellness materials can be converted into a structured, accessible, and user-friendly digital course experience.

Module Week 1: Healthy Lifestyle Foundations
Audience CHANGE Program participants
Estimated Time 20–30 minutes
Format Lesson, checklist, reflection, and quiz

Welcome

Welcome to Week 1 of the CHANGE Program. This module introduces simple, realistic habits that support long-term health. The goal is not perfection. The goal is to begin with small, repeatable actions that can fit into everyday life.

This sample is a course-design mockup. It demonstrates layout, organization, participant instructions, and interactive learning structure for an online Moodle-style course.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the purpose of building small, sustainable health habits.
  • Identify basic nutrition and physical activity goals for the week.
  • Use a simple checklist to track progress.
  • Complete a short knowledge check to review key ideas.

Lesson Content

1. Start Small

Healthy change is easier to maintain when it begins with manageable steps. Instead of trying to change everything at once, choose one or two habits that can be repeated consistently.

2. Nutrition Basics

A balanced plate can include vegetables, fruit, whole grains, and protein. Small changes, such as adding one extra serving of vegetables or planning meals ahead, can support long-term progress.

3. Physical Activity Basics

Movement does not have to be intense to be useful. Walking, stretching, or light exercise can help build routine and confidence, especially when done regularly.

Weekly Checklist

Participants can use this checklist to track simple actions during Week 1.

  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Add one extra fruit or vegetable serving to a meal.
  • Complete at least one 20-minute walk or movement session.
  • Write down one realistic goal for next week.

Reflection Prompt

Before moving on, participants can reflect on the following question:

What is one small health habit you feel confident you can repeat this week?

Mini Quiz

1. What is the main goal of Week 1?

2. Which example best supports a balanced plate?

3. Which activity is a realistic starting point?

Participant Instructions

  1. Read the Week 1 lesson content.
  2. Complete the weekly checklist throughout the week.
  3. Answer the reflection prompt.
  4. Complete the mini quiz before moving to the next module.

Design Notes

This mockup uses clear headings, short sections, accessible language, checklist-style progress tracking, and a simple quiz format. These elements are intended to support participants who may be accessing program materials independently online.
