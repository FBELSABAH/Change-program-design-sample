Welcome to the CHANGE Program

Course modules

1

Start here

This opening module introduces the tone of the program, what support participants can expect, and how to move through the course one step at a time without feeling overwhelmed.

What you will learn
  • How the CHANGE program is organized online.
  • What a realistic pace looks like over the term.
  • Where to find support when motivation drops.
Suggested activity

Write one reason you joined the program and one routine you want to strengthen this month. Keep it simple and personal.

Orientation checkpoint: complete after reading and setting one personal intention.
2

Build a routine that can actually last

Healthy routines work better when they are specific, visible, and manageable. This unit focuses on choosing one or two habits you can repeat consistently instead of trying to change everything at once.

Key ideas
  • Start with a habit that fits your current schedule.
  • Link new actions to routines you already have.
  • Track progress with short weekly reflections.
Practice prompt

Choose one habit for the next seven days, such as walking after lunch or preparing water before bed, and record how often it happened.

Habit checkpoint: identify one weekly goal and one barrier you want to plan around.
3

Nutrition basics without overwhelm

This unit translates nutrition handouts into short digital lessons that focus on balance, planning, and awareness instead of strict rules. Participants can review the material in smaller, easier pieces.

Focus topics
  • Building balanced meals using familiar foods.
  • Planning snacks that support energy and fullness.
  • Reading food labels for a few useful cues.
Reflection question

Which meal is hardest to plan during your week, and what one small change could make it easier next time?

Nutrition checkpoint: review one meal pattern and note one improvement you can repeat.
4

Movement that feels supportive, not punishing

Participants are encouraged to think about movement as a form of care and consistency. The emphasis is on comfort, confidence, and building a routine that matches current ability and energy.

Weekly focus
  • Choose movement options that feel realistic.
  • Warm up, pace yourself, and notice recovery.
  • Celebrate consistency instead of intensity.
Suggested activity

Schedule two short movement sessions this week and reflect on how your mood or energy changed afterward.

Movement checkpoint: plan two sessions and identify one way to make them easier to maintain.
5

Look back, adjust, and keep going

The final module supports reflection instead of perfection. Participants review what has helped, what has been difficult, and what kind of support or next step would help them continue after the course.

Closing prompts
  • What healthy action felt most sustainable?
  • What barrier still needs a better plan?
  • Who or what can support your next step?
Next step

Create a personal follow-up plan with one nutrition goal, one movement goal, and one reminder to check in with yourself again in two weeks.

Reflection checkpoint: complete the quiz module and write one next-step commitment.
6

Quick course review

Use this short quiz to check your understanding of the course themes: starting small, building balanced routines, keeping movement realistic, and planning your next step.

1. What is the best way to begin building a healthy routine in this course?
2. Which idea matches the course approach to nutrition?
3. What is emphasized in the movement module?
4. Why does the final reflection module matter?
